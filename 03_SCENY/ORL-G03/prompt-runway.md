# ORL-G03 — Runway prompt

## Prompt
Cinematic documentary-style visualization of a geological cross-section beneath a modern river landscape in Central Europe. The camera begins just below the present-day ground surface and slowly travels downward through a realistic sequence of sedimentary rock layers. The upper layers are relatively young, loose and heterogeneous. Deeper layers gradually become compact and stratified, transitioning through massive sandstone, limestone, dolomite and mudstone formations. The camera continues downward into a visually distinctive Permian evaporite sequence containing realistic layers of anhydrite, rock salt, dolomite and clay-rich sediments, with natural mineral textures and subtle variations in color and density. The camera then reaches deeper Carboniferous formations composed of sandstone, conglomerate, siltstone and shale-like mudstone. The geological layers must look physically realistic, naturally deposited and three-dimensional, not like a textbook diagram. Strong sense of geological depth and immense geological time. Slow controlled camera movement, subtle parallax between strata, realistic volumetric lighting, cinematic depth, natural rock textures, scientific documentary aesthetic, photorealistic geological visualization, no fantasy elements. The camera gradually slows as it reaches the oldest visible formations, preparing for a seamless dissolve into the next prehistoric environment.
