# ORL-G06 — Runway prompt

## Prompt

Cinematic photorealistic geological time-lapse, natural-history documentary style. Begin with the same humid Lower Carboniferous fluvial landscape established in the previous scene: a shallow stream crossing a broad wet sedimentary plain with restrained ancient vegetation. The camera remains almost completely fixed in the same viewpoint while geological time accelerates dramatically around it. The landscape slowly transforms over immense spans of time: vegetation grows, changes and disappears, the stream gradually shifts its course, water levels change, sediment accumulates across the landscape, old surfaces become buried beneath new layers, erosion reshapes the terrain, and entirely different natural environments gradually replace one another. The transformation must feel continuous and physically grounded, like watching the same location through hundreds of millions of years rather than cutting between unrelated scenes. Sedimentary layers progressively accumulate and subtly become visible beneath the changing surface. Natural changes in atmosphere, lighting, water and vegetation accompany the passage of geological time. The camera remains stable and observational, with only an extremely subtle forward movement or elevation. Serious cinematic natural-history documentary aesthetic, photorealistic geology, realistic environmental transformation, restrained color changes, realistic sediment accumulation and erosion, seamless temporal metamorphosis, immersive, scientifically plausible, no fantasy.
