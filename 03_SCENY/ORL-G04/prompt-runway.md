# ORL-G04 — Runway prompt

## Prompt

Cinematic photorealistic documentary reconstruction of a Lower Carboniferous sedimentary environment in what is now central-western Poland. The scene begins emerging seamlessly from a dark, realistic sandstone and mudstone geological layer, transitioning through a dissolve into a vast humid fluvial and alluvial landscape. Shallow branching streams slowly flow across a broad sedimentary plain. Clear water moves around sand bars, gravel patches and freshly deposited sediment. The landscape is low and open, with wet depressions and naturally eroded banks. Sparse primitive Carboniferous vegetation grows mainly on stable moist ground and along the watercourses, realistic and restrained rather than forming a dense tropical jungle. The environment feels warm, humid and ancient, but scientifically grounded and geologically plausible. Fine sediment is visibly transported by flowing water, with subtle ripples, small channels and freshly deposited sand and mud. Wide cinematic composition, low atmospheric haze, natural daylight, physically realistic water and sediment, detailed rock and soil textures, subtle wind movement in vegetation, slow stabilized aerial camera movement following a shallow stream and gradually revealing the wider landscape. Serious natural-history documentary aesthetic, photorealistic, immersive, restrained, scientifically plausible, no fantasy.
